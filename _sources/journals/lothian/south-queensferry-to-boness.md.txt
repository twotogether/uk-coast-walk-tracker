## Highlights

- Lovely views of the Forth Bridges.
- Walk through the Hopetoun Estate -- chances to see a herd of deer!
- Beautiful walks through woodlands near Abercorn. In summer, these woodlands are full of beautiful foxgloves and wildflowers.
- See Blackness Castle.
- Read information boards on Roman settlements near Bo'ness.

### Cafes and Pubs

- Excellent pubs, bakeries, and cafes in South Queensferry. We love `Dune`, `Mana House`, and `Rogue Bros`.
- `The Lobster Pot` in Blackness is a fabulous pub with quirky decor! We just had drinks, but next time we shall definitely have lunch here.
- Some pubs and cafes in Bo'ness too. We like `Pie King` and `Nosh`. 

### Public Toilets

- Public toilets on Queensferry High Street
- No public toilets in Blackness (2025)
- In Bo'ness, public toilets are on [Register Street](https://maps.app.goo.gl/nLf3B6bicVLHXhMd8) and in the Tesco Superstore
